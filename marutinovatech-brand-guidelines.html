01 — Logo System

The Mark

The Marutinovatech logo is a hexagonal mark combining a stylized M-form with a spark node at its apex — symbolising the convergence of skill, technology, and the nova (explosion) of potential.

07 — Logo Usage

Usage Rules

The mark is an asset. Protect it. These rules keep it strong and consistent across all touchpoints.

↕ 1× cap-height ↕ 1× cap-height ↔ ↔
marutinovatech

Always maintain a minimum clear zone of 1× the cap-height of the wordmark on all sides. Never crowd the mark.

Don't stretch or distort

Never alter the logo's aspect ratio. Scale proportionally from any corner.

Don't recolor the icon

The icon must always appear in Nova Teal (#00E5C8) or in solid white/black only.

Don't use on busy backgrounds

Always place the logo on a solid surface. No patterns, gradients, or photography directly under the mark.

Don't add effects

No drop shadows, glows, outlines, or 3D effects. The mark is flat — always.

Don't separate the mark

Never use the icon and wordmark as two separate unrelated elements in the same layout.

Don't change the typeface

The wordmark always uses Sora with the exact weight split: 300 / 800 / 400. No substitutions.
